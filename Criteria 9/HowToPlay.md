# Knucklebones — How to Play

A strategic dice-placement game for two players, each using a X×Y grid. Players alternate turns rolling a six-sided die and placing it into a chosen column within their grid.

---

## Objective

- Fill your entire X×Y grid.
- At the end, the player with the **highest total score** wins.

---

## Rules & Scoring

- Roll one six-sided die each turn and place it in any column with available space.
- Dice face value equals its base score (e.g., a 5 is worth 5 points).
- Total Score is the total sum of all dice in your grid.
- **Cancellation mechanic**: Placing a die removes all dice of the same face value in the same column of your opponent’s grid.

---

## Gameplay Flow

1. Players alternate turns—roll & place.
2. Use placement and disrupt opponent’s progress.
3. Game ends when **either** grid is completely filled.
4. Compare total scores—higher total wins, even if you filled your grid first.

---


## Quick Reference Table

| Rule               | Details                                                                |
|--------------------|------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| Grid Layout        | X columns × Y rows per player                                          |
| Die Roll           | One six-sided die per turn; choose any open column to place it         |
| Scoring            | Die value                                                              |
| Cancelling         | Matching die removes same-value dice in same column on opponent’s grid |
| Game End Condition | When a board is full; higher total wins                                |
